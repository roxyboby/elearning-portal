Analogy: The Financial Budget

You wouldn't try to manage your money without looking at your bank statement. Wondering where all your money went without any data is just a guessing game. The first step to financial health is understanding exactly where your money is being spent.

Your time is far more valuable than money. A time audit is the equivalent of reviewing your bank statement—it shows you the cold, hard facts of where your time is *actually* going, not where you *think* it's going.

Core Concepts: Data Over Drama

1. Our Perception of Time is Flawed

If you had to guess, how much time did you spend on social media yesterday? How much time did you spend in truly focused work? Studies show we are terrible at estimating these things. We need objective data, not guesses.

2. No Judgment, Just Data

This is the most important rule. The goal of the time audit is NOT to make you feel guilty about watching TV or scrolling on your phone. The goal is to gather honest information. Think of yourself as a scientist observing your own habits. Be neutral, be curious, and above all, be honest.

Your Task: Become a Time Detective

This is the most eye-opening exercise of the course. Do not skip it.

For the next 3 days, you will track your time in 30-minute intervals. It might seem tedious, but the insights you gain will be invaluable.

Action: Download the "Time Audit Log" worksheet. Keep it with you and fill it out throughout the day. At the end of the 3 days, you will have the raw data we need to start making real changes.
